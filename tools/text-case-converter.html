Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert text case?

Paste or type your text, select the case type, and click Convert. The tool will instantly convert your text.

What is Title Case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of each word. Useful for headlines and titles.

Can I convert to sentence case?

Yes, select Sentence case to capitalize the first letter of each sentence automatically.
