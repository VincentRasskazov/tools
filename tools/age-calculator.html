Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my age in years, months, and days?

Simply enter your date of birth and click "Calculate Age." The tool instantly computes your age in years, months, and days, accounting for leap years and month lengths.

Is this age calculator suitable for official purposes?

Yes, this calculator provides a precise age calculation, but always check with the relevant authority if you need certified proof of age for legal or official documents.

Is my date of birth data private?

Absolutely. All calculations are performed in your browser and your data is never sent or stored anywhere.
