Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert degrees to radians?

To convert degrees to radians, multiply the degree value by π/180. This tool does it instantly for you—just enter your value and select the conversion direction.

When should I use radians instead of degrees?

Radians are preferred in advanced mathematics, calculus, and trigonometry, while degrees are more common in everyday use and geometry.
