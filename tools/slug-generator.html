Frequently Asked Questions

What is a slug generator?

A slug generator creates clean, URL-friendly text strings ("slugs") from any input. Slugs are used in web addresses to improve readability and SEO.

How do I create SEO-friendly slugs?

Enter your text and click Generate. The tool will convert it to lowercase, replace spaces with hyphens, and remove special characters.

Why are slugs important for SEO?

SEO-friendly slugs make URLs easier to read and index, improving click-through rates and search rankings.
